# 90-Second Call Outline — 1614 Home Co.
**CURRENT 2026-09-24.** Natural, premium, unhurried. Not a script; hit the beats.

**0:00 — Open (10s)**
“Hi {name}, it's Justin with 1614 Home Co. — you requested a quote {through the mailer / online}. Do you have two minutes?”

**0:10 — Why we exist (15s)**
“We handle the recurring home tasks most people mean to do and don't: filters, leak-sensor checks, the sump pump, smoke and CO checks, seasonal items. Four scheduled visits a year, and everything gets written into a Home Record for the house.”

**0:25 — The home (35s)** — work down the reference sheet, conversationally
“Tell me a little about the house. How many full baths… any half baths? One furnace or two? One water heater? Roughly how big? Sump pump in the basement? Anything unusual — a boiler, a well, a finished basement?”

**1:00 — Where the price lands (15s)**
“Based on that, you'd be in our {up to 3 / 4 / 5}-bath tier — {Founding $699 for year one, if a seat is open / $799 a year} plus a one-time $199 Member Setup, and you can pay annually or monthly. Final pricing reflects your home review, so I'll confirm the exact number in writing.”
*(If any flag: “I want to review that before I give you a number.”)*

**1:15 — Close (15s)**
“You'll have your quote within one business day. I'll send the invoice and the short membership agreement together, and once both are done we'll book your first visit. What's the best email?”

**Tone notes:** listen more than talk; no pressure on Founding; never say inspection / guarantee / protect / prevent; if they ask “is this insurance / a warranty?” — “No — it's a maintenance membership. We do the recurring work on a schedule and document it.”
