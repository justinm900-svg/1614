### What the Department of Taxation decides for you
- **Filing frequency** is assigned by the Department after registration based on expected sales; new small vendors are commonly set to semiannual, but the assignment letter controls. Record whatever the letter says above.
- **Returns are due on the 23rd** of the month following the end of each period, and a return is due even for a $0 period.
- Once licensed, the county auditor / Department may treat you as a registered vendor for building maintenance services; taxability of each 1614 line is a separate question handled in `02-Square/Sales Tax Decision List.md`. **This document does not determine that every membership dollar is taxable.**
